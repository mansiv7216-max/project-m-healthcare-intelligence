Advanced Imaging Coverage Policy

Policy ID: POL-IMG-001

Advanced imaging services such as MRI may be covered when medically necessary
and when all applicable administrative requirements are satisfied.

Coverage requirements:

1. The member must have active eligibility on the date of service.

2. The provider should be in network unless an approved exception applies.

3. The claim must contain a diagnosis supporting medical necessity.

4. Procedure code 70553 requires prior authorization.

5. Procedure code 70551 may require prior authorization depending on the
clinical condition and plan rules.

6. MRI procedures may be considered medically necessary for qualifying
neurological conditions when supporting clinical documentation is available.

If prior authorization is required but not present, the claim should be routed
for additional review and may be denied depending on plan rules.
